Look 01

ReguLearnAI, compliance knowledge assistant

PythonFastAPILangChainFAISSLLaMA 3.3 70BGroqHTML/CSS/JS

A dual-index retrieval-augmented generation system that lets compliance teams ask plain-language questions about financial regulations and internal HR policies, and get confidence-scored answers. Built as a team project with Aadarsh Senapati, Daksh Chaurasia and D. Preethi Sampath.

  • Hybrid retrieval: FAISS vector search combined with a title-token match, with separate indexes for government regulations and company policies.
  • A daily scraper collects circulars from RBI, SEBI, MCA, IRDAI and PFRDA, removes duplicates, and sends them to an approval queue.
  • A Legal-only console to approve or decline each record, train only the approved ones, and keep an audit log, behind role-based access.
